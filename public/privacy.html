Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

NorthList helps you keep track of your job applications. It reads the job mails in your Gmail, finds what each one asks of you, and keeps your applications in one place. This page explains, in plain words, what NorthList reads, what it keeps, who else sees it, and how to remove it.

NorthList is a small app run by one person, Nitin Sirsath. Questions: nitinsirsath8855@gmail.com.

What NorthList reads from your Google account

When you sign in with Google, NorthList gets your name and your email address. It uses them only to sign you in and to show who is signed in.

When you connect Gmail, you give NorthList two permissions:

  • Read your mail (gmail.readonly). NorthList looks at the mail in your inbox from the last 1 to 30 days (you choose), and then at new mail about once an hour.
  • Send mail (gmail.send). NorthList sends a mail only when you write a reply in the app and press Send. It never sends anything by itself.

What NorthList keeps

For every mail in your inbox that it looks at, NorthList keeps:

  • the subject, the sender, and the date;
  • Gmail's id for the mail and its conversation;
  • whether the mail is about a job or not.

It does not keep the text of your mails, their attachments or their preview.

For job mails, NorthList also keeps what its AI found in them: the company, the job title, the recruiter's name and email, interview, test, offer or rejection details, what the mail asks you to do and by when, and a warning if an offer looks like a scam. To show interview times correctly, it may keep a short quote from the mail (up to 280 characters).

From this, NorthList builds your applications, their history and your to-do list.

When you open a mail or a conversation in NorthList, it is read live from Gmail and shown to you. It is not saved.

When you reply from NorthList, the reply goes through your Gmail. NorthList keeps only the fact that you replied and when, and whether its AI read it, not the text.

If you use the optional automation link (Automation page), NorthList keeps what your own automation reports for each job: the company, job title, platform, links, location, work mode, the confirmation text (up to 300 characters), the name of your CV file (never the file) and your answers to the application questions.

AI and your mail

NorthList uses AI to tell job mails from other mail, and to read what a job mail asks of you.

What is sent to the AI:

  • For each new mail: the sender, the subject, Gmail's labels and Gmail's short preview (up to 1,000 characters).
  • For job mails only: the text of the mail (up to 8,000 characters) and the date it arrived, with what is still open in that conversation.
  • When a conversation still needs something from you: the new text of your own reply in it (without the earlier mail it quotes, up to 4,000 characters), so the AI can tell which requests your reply answers.
  • When you ask the AI to write a reply: the whole conversation, both sides. The draft is shown to you and never kept.
  • Mails that Gmail puts under Promotions, Social or Spam are not sent at all (except LinkedIn mail under Social, which often holds job news).

Who reads it: the AI service you choose on the AI providers page.

  • Your own key: your mail goes to your own account with that AI service (today: OpenRouter), under that service's own terms.
  • NorthList free AI: your mail goes to OpenRouter on NorthList's account. OpenRouter passes it to the GPT-6 Luna model on Microsoft Azure servers in the US or Europe.

No training, no keeping: for every request to OpenRouter, NorthList asks for servers that keep nothing ("zero data retention"), so they cannot store your mail or train on it. NorthList itself never uses your mail to train any AI.

How your data is protected

  • Your Gmail access is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
  • AI keys you add are stored encrypted with a separate key, and are never shown again, not even to you.
  • Automation tokens are stored only as a fingerprint (a hash), never in readable form.
  • Passwords (test accounts only) are stored as a salted hash, never in readable form.
  • NorthList's logs leave out mail subjects, senders, mail text, keys and passwords.
  • Everything runs on Render (servers and database) in Singapore.

Who else gets your data

NorthList does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

Only these services take part:

  • Google (sign-in and Gmail), because you connect them.
  • The AI service you choose (see "AI and your mail").
  • Render, which hosts the app and its database.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the app's font. Your browser asks Google for the font file, as on most websites.

Cookies and your browser

  • cc_session keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It cannot be read by scripts on the page.
  • During Google sign-in and Gmail connect, short signed cookies (5 minutes) protect the sign-in from being faked.
  • Your browser also remembers a few display choices: the theme, whether the side menu is open, and the AI reading status.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Gmail on the Gmail sync page. NorthList stops reading your mail and asks Google to cancel its access. You can also remove NorthList's access at any time in your Google account: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Remove an AI key on the AI providers page. The key is deleted at once.
  • Remove an automation token on the Automation page. It stops working at once.
  • Delete everything: write to nitinsirsath8855@gmail.com from the email you sign in with. NorthList deletes your account and all its data, including mails, applications and AI results, within 7 days.

What NorthList has stored stays until you ask for it to be deleted.
