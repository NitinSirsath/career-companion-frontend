From your work
to your feed.

NorthList Posts has no AI of its own. Your AI app does the reading and writing with a small kit of instructions, and the app keeps the drafts until you post.

Nothing goes out
until you tap Post.

Each draft says where it came from and why it is worth a post. Add one line of what you think, pick X or LinkedIn, and post. You get ten seconds to undo.

Posting
Straight to your X account and your LinkedIn profile, through their own APIs. If a post fails, you see why and can try again.
What the app keeps
Your profile, the drafts and your posts. Your X and LinkedIn keys are encrypted. Drafts leave Today after three days.
Your AI's token
Shown once and kept only as a fingerprint. It lasts a year, and you can revoke it on Connect at any time.

Questions

Which AI apps work with it?

Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity have their own setup steps in the kit. Other AI apps that can connect to an MCP server can follow the general steps.

Does it post on its own?

No. Your AI only sends drafts. A post goes out when you tap Post, and you can still undo it for ten seconds.

Is it part of NorthList?

It is a separate app with its own sign-in, at posts.northlist.site. It doesn't read your Gmail or your job search.
