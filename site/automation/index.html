One job at a time,
with you in charge.

Finds jobs that fit

On the sites you choose, it reads each job post and skips jobs that don't match your titles, places or work mode.

Fills in your details

From your profile, saved answers and resume. When a question has no saved answer, it asks you instead of guessing.

Asks before it applies

It shows you the first application of each run and waits for your OK. At a CAPTCHA or security check, it stops.

Counts only what went through

A job is marked applied only after the site confirms it.

The automation is a set of written instructions for your AI app, not a program. Like any AI, it can make mistakes, so watch its first runs.

Every application
in one place.

Connect the automation, and each job it applies to sits in NorthList next to the ones from your Gmail. NorthList never applies to jobs itself.

Automation › Submitted

Each job it applied to, with the links and the answers it sent. If you already track that job, it is added there; when NorthList can't tell, it asks you.

Automation › Not submitted

Jobs it skipped or left for you, with the reason. They never become applications.

Applications

Each job it applied to is also an application. Mail from the company is linked to it and moves it on, the same way as for any other job.

Connecting is optional. Without NorthList, the automation keeps its own daily record in its folder on your computer.

Set up once.

  1. 1

    Get the automation

    Download its folder from GitHub. It's free.

  2. 2

    Open it in your AI app

    It asks for your resume and details once, and keeps them in that folder.

  3. 3

    Connect NorthList

    In NorthList, open Automation › Setup, create a token and add NorthList to your AI app.

  4. 4

    Start a run

    Pick today's sites and how many jobs. Approve the first application, and it goes on one by one.

What NorthList
gets.

Only what your automation reports about each job. Your details and resume stay on your computer.

Everything about your data
For each job it applied to

The company, job title, site, job and apply links, the apply site's address, job ID, where the job was found, location, work mode, time, the site's confirmation, your resume's file name and the answers it sent.

For each job it didn't apply to

The company, job title, site, job link, whether it skipped the job or left it for you, the reason and the time.

Never asked for

Your passwords, or your resume file and its text. NorthList gets only the file's name.

On your computer

Your profile, saved answers, resume and daily record stay in the automation's folder. Your AI app reads them to fill in forms, under its own terms.

If you delete an application, what your automation sent for it goes too. The rest stays until you ask for all your data to be deleted. NorthList's AI sees only counts and site names from it, for your Dashboard tip.

Your token is shown once. NorthList keeps only its fingerprint and first 12 characters. Revoke it on the Automation page and it stops working at once; it stays listed with its name and dates.

Questions

Which job sites does it work on?

It has been tried on LinkedIn Easy Apply, Naukri and Indeed. Its instructions name more sites, but those are not tested yet.

What does it cost?

The automation is free, and NorthList is free while in beta. It runs in your own AI app, under that app's plan.

Do I need NorthList to use it?

No. Without it, the automation keeps its record in a file on your computer. With it, each job sits next to the mails that follow.
