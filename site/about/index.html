Why NorthList exists

A job search lives in your inbox. Applications confirmed, recruiters asking for a call, tests with deadlines, interviews to schedule, and rejections, all mixed in with newsletters and alerts.

Most people keep a spreadsheet next to it. It is out of date the day after you make it, and the mail that needed an answer by Friday is still somewhere in the pile.

NorthList starts from the mails you already get, so your time goes into the next step, not into finding it.

It is a small, independent app run by one person, and free while in beta.

How NorthList handles your mail
  • Read and send, never change

    When you connect Gmail, you give two permissions: read mail, to find your job mails and show you your mail, and send mail, for your replies only. NorthList can’t change or delete anything in your Gmail.

  • Never the text of your mails

    For each job mail, NorthList keeps the subject, sender, date, Gmail’s ids and what the AI found, sometimes with a short quote for interview times. Not the text or the attachments. Ask, and everything is deleted within 7 days.

  • The AI keeps nothing

    NorthList asks the AI service to keep nothing, so it can’t store your mail or train on it.

  • No ads, no tracking

    NorthList doesn’t show ads, sell your data, or use analytics or tracking tools.
