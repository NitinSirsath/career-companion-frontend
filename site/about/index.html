Why NorthList exists

A job search lives in your inbox. Applications confirmed, recruiters asking for a call, tests with deadlines, interviews to schedule, and rejections, all mixed in with newsletters and alerts.

Most people keep a spreadsheet next to it. It is out of date the day after you make it, and the mail that needed an answer by Friday is still somewhere in the pile.

NorthList starts from the mails you already get, so your time goes into the next step, not into finding it.

It is a small, independent app run by one person, and free while in beta.
