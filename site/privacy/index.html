At a glance

How your mail moves

New mail goes from your Gmail to NorthList, and only what the AI needs goes on to the AI service. What comes back becomes your list.

  1. 01
    Your Gmail

    Stays as it is. NorthList reads it and sends only the replies you write. It can't change or delete anything.

  2. New mail, about once an hour
  3. 02
    NorthList

    Skips Promotions, Social and Spam, except LinkedIn mail under Social. Sends only what the AI needs.

  4. Sender, subject, labels, preview; text of job mails
  5. 03
    AI service

    Reads it and answers. NorthList asks it to keep nothing (zero data retention), so it can't store your mail or train on it.

  6. Company, role, next step
  7. 04
    Your list

    Subject, sender, date and what the AI found. Never the text of the mail.

When you open a mail, NorthList reads it live from Gmail and shows it to you. Its servers don't save it.

What NorthList reads

When you sign in with Google, NorthList gets your name and email address. It uses them to sign you in and to show who is signed in, and your first name to sign AI reply drafts. When you connect Gmail, you give two permissions:

Read your mailgmail.readonly

NorthList looks at the mail in your inbox from the last 1 to 30 days (you choose), then at new mail about once an hour.

Send mailgmail.send

Only when you write a reply in NorthList and press Send. It never sends anything by itself.

On Google's screen these appear as "View your email messages and settings" and "Send email on your behalf". NorthList uses them only as described here.

NorthList also keeps the address of the Gmail account you connect, to show which account it reads and to tell your own mails from other people's. It keeps the address after you disconnect too, so that only the same account can connect again.

What NorthList keeps

From your job mails, NorthList builds your applications, their history and your to-do list.

In full, NorthList keeps:

  • For a job mailThe subject, the sender, the date, and Gmail's id for the mail and its conversation.
  • What the AI found in itThe company, the job title, the recruiter's name and email, interview, test, offer or rejection details, what the mail asks you to do and by when, and a warning if an offer looks like a scam.
  • A short quoteUp to 280 characters, may be kept to show interview times correctly.
  • Two yes/no notesWhether the mail was sent to many people as a mailing, and whether it says not to reply.
  • Your replies to job mailsOnly that you replied, when, and whether the AI read the reply. Not the text.
  • Mail that is not about a jobOnly Gmail's id, its date and that it is not a job mail, so it is not read twice. No subject, no sender. Deleted after 35 days.
  • Inbox mails checked each dayOnly the number, no mail details. Kept until you delete your account.
  • When you last used the appUpdated at most once an hour, and which messages from NorthList's owner you closed.
  • If the owner suspends your accountThe reason you are shown and, if you ask to resume it, the text you send, until it is resumed.
  • If you turn on alertsThat device's push address and keys, until you turn alerts off or sign out there.
  • If you use the automation linkFor each job it applied to: company, job title, job site, the job's link and ID, where the job was found, the link and address of the page where it applied, location, work mode, when it applied, the confirmation text (up to 300 characters), the name of your CV file (never the file) and your answers to the application questions.
  • Job portal accountsFor Workday and other company portals: the sign-in page, login email, password, whether the email was checked and when the account was made, so you can sign in from NorthList. Never your passwords for LinkedIn and other job sites you sign in to yourself.
  • For each job it did not apply toCompany, job title, job site, the job's link, whether it skipped the job or left it for you, why (up to 1,000 characters) and when.

Everything else stays until you ask for it to be deleted.

What it never keeps

  • The text of your mailsTheir attachments or Gmail's preview.
  • A mail you openIt is read live from Gmail and shown to you. NorthList's servers don't save it. Your browser keeps a copy for offline pages, on your device only, for up to 7 days, and removes it when you sign out.
  • The text of your repliesAnd anything about your replies to other mail.
  • AI draftsA draft is shown to you and never kept.
  • A reply you haven't sentIt stays in your browser only, until you send it, discard it or sign out.

Which AI service

NorthList free AI reads your mail from the moment you connect Gmail; the line under the Connect Gmail button says so. You can connect your own key on the AI providers page at any time.

NorthList free AI

Your mail goes to OpenRouter on NorthList's account. OpenRouter passes it to the GPT-6 Luna model on Microsoft Azure servers in the US or Europe.

Your own key

Your mail goes to your own account with that AI service (today: OpenRouter), under that service's own terms.

No training, no keeping. For every request to OpenRouter, NorthList asks for servers that keep nothing ("zero data retention"), so they cannot store your mail or train on it. NorthList itself never uses your mail to train any AI.

How it is protected

  • Gmail accessYour Gmail access is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
  • AI keysAI keys you add are encrypted with a separate key, and are never shown again, not even to you.
  • Job portal passwordsEncrypted with their own key (AES-256-GCM), never written to logs, and shown only to you when you tap Show. Deleting the last application that used one, or all your data, removes it.
  • Automation tokensStored as a fingerprint (a hash) and their first 12 characters, so you can tell them apart. The full token is never stored.
  • LogsNorthList's logs leave out mail subjects, senders, mail text, keys and passwords.
  • Admin pageOnly NorthList's owner can open it. It shows account facts and counts, never the text, subject or sender of any mail.
  • Where it runsThe app runs on Render and its database on Supabase, both in Singapore.

Who else takes part

NorthList does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking tools. Only these services take part:

  • GoogleFor sign-in and Gmail, because you connect them.
  • The AI service you chooseAs described above.
  • RenderHosts the app.
  • SupabaseHosts the app's database.
  • Google FontsServes the font. Your browser asks Google for the font file, as on most websites.
  • Your browser's push serviceOnly if you turn on alerts. It carries each alert, such as "Acme: Reply with your availability". The alert is encrypted, so the push service cannot read it.

Your choices

  • Disconnect GmailOn the Gmail sync page. NorthList stops reading your mail and asks Google to cancel its access. You can also remove access in your Google account.
  • Delete a conversationFrom its "…" menu. Its subjects, senders, AI results and to-dos go at once. Gmail's ids and dates stay for 35 days, so those mails are not read again. Your Gmail is not changed.
  • Block a companyFrom a mail's "…" menu, or when you delete a conversation. NorthList then does not read or keep new mail from its domain. LinkedIn and mail providers anyone can use can't be blocked. Unblock it in Mails › Restricted.
  • Delete an applicationFrom its "…" menu. Its to-dos, history and what your automation reported for it go at once; its mails stay in Mails.
  • Remove an AI keyOn the AI providers page. It is deleted at once.
  • Turn off alertsOn the Account page, on each phone or computer. Signing out there turns them off too.
  • Revoke an automation tokenOn the NorthList Apply page. It stops working at once and stays listed with its name and dates.
  • Jobs your automation did not apply toAnd jobs you ignore in Waiting for a match, can't be deleted one by one. They stay until you delete everything.
  • Delete everythingWrite to northlist.site@gmail.com from the email you sign in with. Your account and all its data are deleted within 7 days.
