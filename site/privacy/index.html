At a glance

How your mail moves

New mail goes from your Gmail to NorthList, and only what the AI needs goes on to the AI service. What comes back becomes your list.

What NorthList reads

When you sign in with Google, NorthList gets your name and email address. It uses them to sign you in and to show who is signed in, and your first name to sign AI reply drafts. When you connect Gmail, you give two permissions:

Read your mailgmail.readonly

NorthList looks at the mail in your inbox from the last 1 to 30 days (you choose), then at new mail about once an hour.

Send mailgmail.send

Only when you write a reply in NorthList and press Send. It never sends anything by itself.

On Google's screen these appear as "View your email messages and settings" and "Send email on your behalf". NorthList uses them only as described here.

NorthList also keeps the address of the Gmail account you connect, to show which account it reads and to tell your own mails from other people's. It keeps the address after you disconnect too, so that only the same account can connect again.

What NorthList keeps

From your job mails, NorthList builds your applications, their history and your to-do list. It keeps:

  • For a job mail: the subject, the sender, the date, and Gmail's id for the mail and its conversation.
  • What the AI found in it: the company, the job title, the recruiter's name and email, interview, test, offer or rejection details, what the mail asks you to do and by when, and a warning if an offer looks like a scam.
  • A short quote, up to 280 characters, may be kept to show interview times correctly.
  • Your replies to job mails: only that you replied, when, and whether the AI read the reply. Not the text.
  • Mail that is not about a job: only Gmail's id, its date and that it is not a job mail, so it is not read twice. No subject, no sender. Deleted after 35 days.
  • How many inbox mails it checked each day: only the number, no mail details. Kept until you delete your account.
  • If you turn on alerts: that device's push address and keys, until you turn alerts off or sign out there.
  • If you use the automation link, for each job it applied to: company, job title, job site, the job's link and ID, where the job was found, the link and address of the page where it applied, location, work mode, when it applied, the confirmation text (up to 300 characters), the name of your CV file (never the file) and your answers to the application questions.
  • For each job it did not apply to: company, job title, job site, the job's link, whether it skipped the job or left it for you, why (up to 1,000 characters) and when.

Everything else stays until you ask for it to be deleted.

What it never keeps

  • The text of your mails, their attachments or Gmail's preview.
  • A mail you open. It is read live from Gmail and shown to you. NorthList's servers don't save it. Your browser keeps a copy for offline pages, on your device only, for up to 7 days, and removes it when you sign out.
  • The text of your replies, and anything about your replies to other mail.
  • AI drafts. A draft is shown to you and never kept.
  • A reply you haven't sent. It stays in your browser only, until you send it, discard it or sign out.

Which AI service

NorthList free AI

Your mail goes to OpenRouter on NorthList's account. OpenRouter passes it to the GPT-6 Luna model on Microsoft Azure servers in the US or Europe.

Your own key

Your mail goes to your own account with that AI service (today: OpenRouter), under that service's own terms.

No training, no keeping. For every request to OpenRouter, NorthList asks for servers that keep nothing ("zero data retention"), so they cannot store your mail or train on it. NorthList itself never uses your mail to train any AI.

How it is protected

  • Your Gmail access is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
  • AI keys you add are encrypted with a separate key, and are never shown again, not even to you.
  • Automation tokens are stored as a fingerprint (a hash) and their first 12 characters, so you can tell them apart. The full token is never stored.
  • NorthList's logs leave out mail subjects, senders, mail text, keys and passwords.
  • The app runs on Render and its database on Supabase, both in Singapore.

Who else takes part

NorthList does not sell your data, does not show ads, and does not use analytics or tracking tools. Only these services take part:

  • Google, for sign-in and Gmail, because you connect them.
  • The AI service you choose, as described above.
  • Render, which hosts the app.
  • Supabase, which hosts the app's database.
  • Google Fonts, which serves the font. Your browser asks Google for the font file, as on most websites.
  • Your browser's push service, only if you turn on alerts. It carries each alert, such as "Acme: Reply with your availability". The alert is encrypted, so the push service cannot read it.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Gmail on the Gmail sync page. NorthList stops reading your mail and asks Google to cancel its access. You can also remove access in your Google account.
  • Delete a conversation from its "…" menu. Its subjects, senders, AI results and to-dos go at once. Gmail's ids and dates stay for 35 days, so those mails are not read again. Your Gmail is not changed.
  • Block a company from a mail's "…" menu, or when you delete a conversation. NorthList then does not read or keep new mail from its domain. LinkedIn and mail providers anyone can use can't be blocked. Unblock it in Mails › Restricted.
  • Delete an application from its "…" menu. Its to-dos, history and what your automation reported for it go at once; its mails stay in Mails.
  • Remove an AI key on the AI providers page. It is deleted at once.
  • Turn off alerts on the Account page, on each phone or computer. Signing out there turns them off too.
  • Revoke an automation token on the NorthList Apply page. It stops working at once and stays listed with its name and dates.
  • Jobs your automation did not apply to, and jobs you ignore in Waiting for a match, can't be deleted one by one. They stay until you delete everything.
  • Delete everything: write to nitinsirsath8855@gmail.com from the email you sign in with. Your account and all its data are deleted within 7 days.
