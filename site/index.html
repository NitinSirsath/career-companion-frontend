How your mail is handled
  • Read and send, never change

    Two Gmail permissions: read, to find your job mails, and send, for your replies only. Nothing in your Gmail can be changed or deleted.

  • Never the text of your mails

    For each job mail NorthList keeps the subject, sender, date and what the AI found. Not the text, not the attachments.

  • The AI keeps nothing

    The AI service is asked to keep nothing, so it can’t store your mail or train on it.

  • No ads, no tracking

    No ads, no selling your data, no analytics or tracking tools.
